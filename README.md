# Okay so basically... welcome to our little corner of the internet 🤍

Listen, regular note apps or generic photo dumps just weren't cutting it for how much Nicky means to me, so I sat down and built this whole digital memory vault instead lol. 

Consider this our living scrapbook: tracking every single breath and second since **September 20, 2026**, keeping all our messy polaroid keepsakes intact, and archiving the kind of chaotic, warm moments that make life actually fun.

---

### What's actually in here? (A quick yap session)

- **The Live Day Counter**: Yup, it counts every single day, hour, minute, and second since day one. No more guessing "how long have we been together again?" because math is now automated.
- **The Guy & The Girl**: A breakdown of our little universe. Basically me being the quiet INFJ navigator who drives and makes manual brew coffee, versus Nicky—the sweet, sunset-loving ENFP girl who somehow makes every single second 10x brighter (and judges my coffee orders).
- **Our Track ("Until I Found You")**: Embedded Stephen Sanchez right into the page. Because honestly? We screamed this in the car way too many times during midnight highway drives for it not to have its own shrine.
- **The Timeline of Lore**:
  - *That Internship Era*: The sneaky glance-across-the-desk phase, turning stressful office deadlines into lunch break dates and coffee talks that somehow lasted until the cafe literally told us to leave.
  - *RamenYA! Plaza Asia Tasikmalaya*: Our ultimate comfort routine when adulting gets overwhelming. Warm broth, silly conversations, and forgetting the rest of the world exists.
  - *Future Shenanigans*: Kyoto in autumn, leaf-peeping, decorating our dream study room, and staying best friends for the rest of forever.
- **"Love Wins All" Polaroid Collage**: Made this inspired by retro camcorders and layered editorial film negatives. Ripped edges, soft focus, scattered typewriter words, and zero cringe corporate vibes.
- **Memories Vault**: Click the polaroids or the vault button and boom—our favorite unhinged candid smiles, bridge walks, and forest adventures all in one clean gallery modal.
- **The Heart Shower Button**: If you click the heart at the bottom, it literally rains floating hearts all over the screen. Because why not.

---

### Tech stuff (for the nerds)

- Built with clean **HTML5**, **Vanilla JavaScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**.
- Typography: *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, and *VT323* (for that retro typewriter lyric aesthetic).
- No heavy frameworks, zero bloat, fast load times, and ready to deploy straight to Vercel on every commit.

---

### Running it yourself

If you're testing this locally:

```bash
# fire it up with any simple static server
npx serve .
# or
python -m http.server 3000
```

Head over to `http://localhost:3000` and enjoy.
